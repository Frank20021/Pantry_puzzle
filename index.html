About Pantry Puzzle

The Problem

Food waste is a global crisis affecting our environment and economy:

  • 1.3 billion tons of food is wasted annually worldwide
  • 40% of food in the US goes uneaten
  • Average household wastes $1,500 worth of food each year
  • Food waste contributes to 8% of global greenhouse gas emissions

Our Mission

Pantry Puzzle aims to transform how we use leftover ingredients by:

  • Helping users create delicious meals from available ingredients
  • Reducing food waste through smart recipe suggestions
  • Making sustainable cooking accessible and enjoyable
  • Empowering users to make the most of their pantry

Key Benefits

  • Save Money: Reduce grocery bills by using ingredients you already have
  • Reduce Waste: Minimize food waste and its environmental impact
  • Discover Recipes: Get creative with AI-generated recipe suggestions
  • Learn Cooking: Improve cooking skills with detailed instructions
  • Plan Better: Make informed decisions about food purchases

Impact

Our users have achieved remarkable results:

  • 25% reduction in food waste
  • $370 average annual savings per person
  • 90% of users report improved cooking confidence
  • 85% reduction in expired food items
