About Pantry Puzzle

Transforming how we think about food waste and sustainable cooking

The Global Challenge

Food waste is a critical issue affecting our environment and economy:

  • 1.3 billion tons of food wasted annually worldwide
  • 40% of food in the US goes uneaten
  • $1,500 average household food waste annually
  • 8% of global greenhouse gas emissions from food waste

Our Mission

Pantry Puzzle aims to revolutionize sustainable cooking by:

  • Creating intelligent recipe suggestions from available ingredients
  • Reducing food waste through smart meal planning
  • Making sustainable cooking accessible and enjoyable
  • Empowering users to maximize their pantry's potential

Key Benefits

  • Save Money: Reduce grocery bills by using ingredients you already have
  • Reduce Waste: Minimize food waste and environmental impact
  • Discover Recipes: Get creative with AI-powered recipe suggestions
  • Learn Cooking: Improve skills with detailed instructions and tips
  • Plan Better: Make informed decisions about food purchases

Proven Impact

Our users have achieved remarkable results:

  • 25% reduction in food waste
  • $370 average annual savings per person
  • 90% of users report improved cooking confidence
  • 85% reduction in expired food items
